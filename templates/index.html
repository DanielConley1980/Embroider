Background

Higher removes more of the background. Works best on plain or solid backgrounds.

Reduce palette

Rebuilds your image from the fewest threads that keep its character — the most contrasting colours, one thread each. Everything downstream builds on this.

Lower keeps subtle shade differences; higher suppresses near-identical colours and merges them into one thread.

Vectorise

Traces the reduced design into smooth, scalable shapes. Higher smoothing drops small specks for a tidier logo.
